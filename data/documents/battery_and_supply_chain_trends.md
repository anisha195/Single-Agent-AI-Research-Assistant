# Global Battery Economics, Chemistry Trends, and Supply Chain (2020-2025)

## Battery Pack Price Trends
- **2020:** Average volume-weighted lithium-ion battery pack price was $140 per kilowatt-hour ($140/kWh).
- **2021:** Pack prices fell to $132/kWh as gigafactory manufacturing efficiencies expanded.
- **2022:** Prices temporarily spiked to $151/kWh due to surging raw material costs (lithium carbonate peaked at over $70,000/ton, alongside spike in nickel and cobalt prices).
- **2023:** Significant price declines resumed, falling 14% to $139/kWh as massive mining capacity came online in China, Australia, and South America.
- **2024:** Lithium-ion battery pack prices dropped to record lows of $115/kWh globally, with cell-level prices dropping below $80/kWh for LFP chemistries.
- **2025 Outlook:** Industry consensus estimates battery pack prices breaching the psychological $100/kWh threshold for standard-range packs, reaching cost parity with internal combustion engines (ICE) without government subsidies.

## Chemistry Evolution: LFP vs. High-Nickel NMC
1. **Lithium Iron Phosphate (LFP):**
   - Market share expanded from under 20% in 2020 to >45% globally in 2024/2025.
   - Advantages: 20-30% lower cost, zero cobalt and zero nickel, superior thermal stability and fire resistance, longer cycle life (3,000+ cycles vs 1,500 cycles).
   - Drawbacks: Lower gravimetric energy density (160-190 Wh/kg pack vs 240-270 Wh/kg for high-nickel).
   - Dominance: Standard in entry-level US vehicles (Tesla Model 3 RWD, Ford Mustang Mach-E Standard Range) and over 85% of Indian electric two-wheelers and four-wheelers.

2. **Nickel Manganese Cobalt (NMC 811):**
   - Retains dominance in long-range premium vehicles, heavy-duty trucks, and cold-climate operating environments.
   - Reduced cobalt intensity from 1:1:1 ratios to 8:1:1 and 9:0.5:0.5 to mitigate human rights and price volatility risks.

3. **Emerging Technologies (2025 Horizon):**
   - **Sodium-ion (Na-ion):** Rapid pilot production in China and India for urban micro-mobility; zero lithium dependency, high performance down to -20°C, and pack costs approaching $40-60/kWh at volume.
   - **Semi-Solid State & Solid-State Batteries:** First commercial deployment in luxury halo EVs (e.g. Nio 150 kWh pack), offering 360 Wh/kg energy density and 1,000 km driving ranges.

## Critical Mineral Supply Chain Concentration
- **Refining Capacity:** China processes approximately 65-70% of global lithium, 70% of cobalt, 95% of battery-grade manganese, and over 90% of anode-grade spherical graphite.
- **Geopolitical Diversification Initiatives:**
  - United States: Inflation Reduction Act Section 45X production tax credits incentivizing domestic gigafactories (Tennessee, Georgia, Michigan, Nevada).
  - India: Discovery of lithium reserves in Jammu & Kashmir and domestic ACC PLI auctions awarding 30 GWh of battery manufacturing capacity to local conglomerates (Reliance New Energy, Ola Electric, Rajesh Exports).
