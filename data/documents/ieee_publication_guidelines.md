# IEEE Guidelines for AI Research Assistants & Autonomous Literature Survey

## Purpose for AI Research Agents
This internal manual guides the autonomous research assistant when evaluating, citing, verifying, and comparing academic literature conforming to IEEE standards. When users request literature surveys, manuscript preparation guidance, or IEEE format verification, the agent must reference this document.

## Critical IEEE Standards for Research Assistants to Enforce
1. **Citation & Reference Protocol:**
   - IEEE uses numeric citations in order of appearance in square brackets: e.g., `[1]`, `[2]`, `[3]–[5]`.
   - Never invent references or assign fictitious DOIs.
   - Standard format for journal papers: `[1] J. K. Author, "Title of paper," IEEE Trans. Autom. Control, vol. 50, no. 3, pp. 240–252, Mar. 2024, doi: 10.1109/TAC.2024.1234567.`
   - Standard format for conferences: `[2] A. B. Author, "Title of conference paper," in Proc. IEEE ICRA, 2024, pp. 1045–1052.`

2. **Abstract Verification Criteria:**
   - Length: Strictly 150 to 250 words for standard transactions and journals (100–150 words for brief letters).
   - Self-contained: Must contain zero citations, zero figure/table references, and zero undefined acronyms.
   - Mandatory elements: Problem statement, proposed methodology, quantitative findings, and technological significance.

3. **Manuscript Layout Rules:**
   - 2-column format on US Letter or A4 with 0.25-inch column separation.
   - Typography: Times New Roman, 10 pt regular for body text; 8–9 pt for table data, footnotes, and references.
   - Equations: Numbered consecutively flush with right margin `(1)`, `(2)`; variables italicized, matrices/vectors bold.

4. **Plagiarism, Originality & Generative AI Policy:**
   - CrossCheck (iThenticate) similarity screening threshold: text recycling or self-plagiarism without explicit citation is prohibited.
   - AI Authorship Rule: Generative AI tools (e.g., Gemini, ChatGPT) cannot be listed as authors. The use of AI tools in research or drafting must be explicitly disclosed in the Methodology or Acknowledgments section.
